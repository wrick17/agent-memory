## Remaining material issues

**A. P2: the generation allocator can roll back.** Line 1196 puts the sole counter in `AccountStore`; line 1647 restores that store from an older export. Example: export at counter 40, allocate live generation 41 for node N, restore the account, retain 41 through reconciliation, then allocate scratch generation 41. Both mutate `g41:N`; scratch cleanup deletes the live vector. Ownership reconciliation does not recover an allocation watermark. Persist reservations outside account rollback, or introduce a fresh allocation epoch. Require an account-restore-then-scratch check that preserves post-export live vectors and never reuses allocated identities.

**B. P2: digest saturation still permits acknowledged loss.** Lines 1085-1099 put an unrestricted input range into one 8 KB digest, using a schema with `dropped` counts. Consider one range containing 100 independent corrections whose distinct literal identifiers alone exceed 8 KB. A valid capped digest must omit some. Line 1100 then advances completeness and deletes the retained full source because that digest was stored. Split into bounded source subranges or digest parts, and leave overflow/incomplete ranges pending until every part is durable. Test saturation without inline consolidation. Different-hash model retries are refused safely; regeneration itself is not deterministic replay proof.

**C. P1: undo payloads survive a cross-profile wipe.** Move work node N to `personal`, then update its body. Line 1329 stores the prior work body in `personal.activity.undo`. The purge list at 1053 removes N and its texts, revisions, requests, evidence, vectors and archive, but leaves that activity payload. The fresh export at 1056 retains it. Purge or redact content-bearing activity/undo rows targeting purged ids, preserving only content-free audit facts. Add this move/update/wipe case and scan the fresh export for the old body.
